# Participant Information and Consent — Protocol v1

**Status: FROZEN FOR PILOT**
**Study title:** HaUI Compass controlled thesis pilot
**Session duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Participant plan:** target 12; acceptable range 10–15; fewer than 10 is exploratory only

## Participant information

You are invited to take part in a minimal-risk personal usability pilot evaluating the usability and perceived usefulness of the HaUI Compass prototype. You will use a web application with fictional courses, assignments, and study tasks, complete short tasks and questionnaires, and join a short interview. One session will take approximately 30–45 minutes.

Taking part is voluntary. You may decline any question, skip a task, or stop at any time without giving a reason and without penalty. Participation or non-participation has no effect on grades, university standing, or access to services. No HaUI or LMS credential is required.

The study collects a pseudonymous participant ID (`P01`, `P02`, …), task success/failure, task times, observed UI errors/navigation/assistance, questionnaire answers, comprehension responses, and interview notes. It does not require real academic records. It will not collect passwords, HaUI/LMS credentials, student IDs, financial information, health information, or unnecessary identifying information.

The consent form and analysis data will be separated. Raw/pseudonymous study data will be retained for six months after thesis/project completion and then deleted. Aggregated anonymized results may remain in the thesis/project report. Results will be reported in aggregate or with de-identified quotations. There is a small risk that discussing study organisation may feel inconvenient; you may pause, skip, or stop.

**Ethics status: Minimal-risk personal usability pilot. Formal institutional ethics approval has not been obtained.** If an institution later requires ethics approval for formal thesis use, that approval must be obtained before collecting data under that institutional study.
