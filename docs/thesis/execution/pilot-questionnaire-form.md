## Response instruction

For custom items, select one: 1 Strongly disagree, 2 Disagree, 3 Neither agree nor disagree, 4 Agree, 5 Strongly agree, or Prefer not to answer. “Prefer not to answer” is recorded as missing, not as a midpoint.

## Open-ended questions

1. What, if anything, made the recommendation explanation easier or harder to understand?
2. What, if anything, made the revised plan feel useful or not useful?
3. Is there any information you expected to see but could not find?

## Interview prompts

1. What was easiest during the workflow?
2. What was confusing or took more effort than expected?
3. How did you understand why the system selected the next task?
4. How realistic did the weekly plan feel for this fictional workload?
5. What did you expect the replanning step to do after the change?
6. What information was missing, if any?
7. Would you consider using a tool like this for your actual coursework? Why or why not?
8. What would stop you from using it?
