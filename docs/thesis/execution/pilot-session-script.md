# Pilot Session Script — Protocol v1

**Researcher-only.** One moderated session lasts 30–45 minutes. Use participant IDs `P01`–`P15` only. Read the bold text verbatim where provided; do not coach a participant toward a correct action.

## Before the participant arrives

Complete the pre-session checklist, reset the canonical fixture, open the prototype, and prepare a blank observation sheet, questionnaire form, and scenario sheet for the assigned ID.

## Welcome and consent (about 3 minutes)

**“Thank you for taking part. This is a minimal-risk personal usability pilot of the HaUI Compass prototype. Today uses fictional academic data, not your real coursework. The session takes about 30 to 45 minutes. Taking part is voluntary: you may skip a question, skip a task, pause, or stop at any time without consequence. We do not need or collect passwords, LMS credentials, student IDs, or real academic records.”**

**“Please review the participant information and consent material. Do you consent to participate?”** Record consent according to the approved local process, separately from the observation record. If consent is not given, stop the session.

## Study introduction (about 3 minutes)

**“I will show the available pages and ordinary controls, but I will not explain how to complete the tasks or which answer is correct. Please say what you are thinking if you are comfortable doing so. I may take notes, but I will not interrupt unless you ask for clarification, there is a technical problem, or you choose to stop.”**

Give the neutral 3–5 minute interface orientation defined in Protocol v1: identify pages and ordinary controls only. Do not reveal recommendations, reasons, expected plan changes, or task answers.

## Fictional-scenario handoff

Give the participant [pilot-scenario-sheet.md](pilot-scenario-sheet.md). Say:

**“This is the same fictional scenario used for every participant. Please use only this scenario while completing the tasks. I will read each task aloud and you may also read it yourself.”**

## Participant tasks

Read T1–T10 exactly as written in [pilot-participant-tasks.md](../pilot-participant-tasks.md), one at a time. Start and stop the task timer at the participant's first/last task-directed action. Record events on the observation sheet. Apply the fixed facilitator change before T9 only, as defined in the scenario sheet.

### Neutral assistance rules

- A participant may ask for the task instruction to be reread; reread it verbatim and record one assistance event.
- Do not name a page, button, field, task, recommendation, reason, or expected answer.
- Do not correct an input or tell the participant whether an action is right.
- For a technical failure, pause timing, record the incident, restore the known state, and repeat only the affected task. Do not count the incident as a participant error.
- If a participant stops/skips a task, record abandonment or not attempted; do not infer success or failure.

## Questionnaire transition (about 6 minutes)

After the specified tasks, say: **“Please answer these questions based on your experience of this fictional scenario. There are no right or wrong opinions. You may select ‘Prefer not to answer’ for custom questions.”**

Administer the unmodified SUS and then the separate custom items in [pilot-questionnaire-form.md](pilot-questionnaire-form.md). Do not explain or reword SUS items.

## Interview transition (about 5–8 minutes)

Say: **“I will now ask a few open questions about your experience. Please describe what you experienced; I am interested in both positive and difficult parts.”** Ask the interview prompts verbatim and use only neutral follow-ups such as “Can you tell me more?”

## Debrief

**“Thank you. This session used fictional data. Your pseudonymous study record will be retained for six months after thesis/project completion and then deleted; aggregated anonymized results may remain in the report. Do you have any questions or anything you would like to add?”**

Save records only under the participant ID, reset the environment, and complete the post-session checklist.
