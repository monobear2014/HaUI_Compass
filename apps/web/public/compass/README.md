# HaUI Compass visuals

`study-hero.png` is an original illustration generated on 2026-10-06 with the
built-in imagegen tool. It replaces the Cursus-branded videos and posters.
The compass mark is project-native SVG in `src/components/compass/primitives.tsx`.

Final prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: HaUI Compass student learning website hero background, landscape 16:9. Create an original polished editorial hand-drawn illustration of a Vietnamese university student studying at a laptop in a calm modern campus library in Hanoi. Warm cream paper texture, muted navy and sage accents, soft afternoon window light. Student and desk occupy the right half, left half has quiet uncluttered negative space for white website headline overlay. Laptop screen has abstract weekly calendar blocks, notebook and a small compass on desk, bookshelves and greenery in background. Inviting, focused, contemporary student product, tasteful linework and soft shading, not childish or technical dashboard. No letters or text anywhere, no logos, no mascots, no brand marks, no watermark. Do not reproduce Cursus or any university seal. Full bleed wide image.

Font files in `/public/fonts` are Manrope and Be Vietnam Pro, with their SIL OFL
licenses. The public pages borrow the requested reference's visual structure,
but their brand, copy, illustrations and student entry flow are HaUI Compass.
